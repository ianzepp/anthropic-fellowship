# Why this program

Draft for the form. 1,126 characters.

I was out of tech for several years. Claude Code brought me back. What I wanted was to work the intent on problems I do not fully know how to implement, and have the model carry the implementation.

Early in that work I designed and ran behavioral pressure trials, in January and March 2026, then audited the grader and re-ran them in October against 43 current models. I wanted to know how breakable the constraints are and where the internal modeling comes from. That question is older than the models. Peter Watts's Blindsight is about consciousness as against intelligence, and the Chinese room. I used to reread it almost every year. I have an internal pattern matcher and a rulebook, and without enough observation I do not know which rule applies. Early language models worked in a way I recognized. Iain M. Banks's Culture novels are the other half: what a much more advanced mind becomes, and how it sits inside a society.

Since August 2025 that is also how I have built. I direct the agents. I do not type the implementation. Radix, the Faber compiler, is about 1.37 million lines of code, and I wrote none of them.
